# Steering baseline and MIT harness: what is public (checked 2026-11-15)

Supporting detail for the README. Read from the public repos and paper by a research agent; items marked unverified were not confirmed.

## Contrastive weight steering (Fierro & Roger, arXiv 2511.05408; github.com/safety-research/weight-steering)

- Data: persona_vectors questions (40 evil questions, 20 for extraction, 20 held out), 5 positive and 5 negative system prompts, 10 samples per pair, filtered by GPT-4.1-mini. Released as `cfierro/pv-prompts-evil` and `cfierro/pv-prompts-non-evil` (779 rows each, generated by Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, system prompt already stripped).
- Training (`axolotl_configs/qwen-2.5-7b/lora-evil-pv_prompts.yml`): standard LoRA r=32, alpha=16, all linear layers, embeddings and lm_head fully trained, 8-bit base, lr 1e-5 (5e-5 gave a better evil vector, App. B.2), batch 8, 100 steps, merged.
- Edit: dense delta over every float tensor of the merged checkpoints, θ_ft + k·(θ_pos − θ_neg) (`task_vectors.py`). No layer selection. Their "positive" is evil, so our aligned-minus-misaligned vector is −w_evil.
- k: published steered checkpoints exist for k = 1–20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30 (`coastalcph/Qwen2.5-7B-plus-{k}t_diff_pv_evil`). Merged fine-tunes are public too (`coastalcph/Qwen2.5-7B-pv-prompts-evil`, `-non-evil`, about 15 GB each).
- Their evil result exists only as plots (no table): target numbers unverified.
- Licence: the repo and the model cards declare none; the paper is CC BY 4.0.

## MIT harness (github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs, "Sparks of Weight Space Programming")

- Agent: `gpt-6-astra` through `codex exec`, all tools off; full prompt in `exp8_math/runs/p50c/message.txt`.
- Kernel: persistent CPU Python, no internet; helpers `NAMES`, `load`, `load_original`, `save_edit`, `drop_edit`, `edited()`, `tok`; 900 s and 96 GB per run, at most 300 runs.
- Budget: counted in forward requests; one pass is up to 512 generations or 1,024 teacher-forced texts (not 64 sequences, as the proposal says).
- No-gradient rule: stated in the prompt; gradients disabled; the model runs only through the forward tool. `save_edit` refuses the final norm, embeddings / lm_head and whole-tensor rescaling.
- The Experiment 8 harness code itself (sandbox, forward-pass server) is not released, so enforcement beyond the prompt is unverified and we would rebuild it from the prompt and logs.
- Release format: per run `turns/tNNN/{prompt.txt,answer.md,events.jsonl,run.json}`, `tool_calls.jsonl`, `requests/`, `edit/{manifest.json,edit.npz}`. Logs CC BY 4.0, scripts MIT.
- Scores (MATH-500; unedited / 10 / 25 / 50 passes): 7.9 / 12.3 / 14.1 / 48.1.
